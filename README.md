# PortaBooth

A vocal booth that runs in your browser. Load an instrumental, record your voice over it while the beat plays in your headphones, stack takes on separate tracks, and export the mix as a WAV. Nothing to install.

- **Load beat**, or drag an audio file onto the tracks
- **R** to record (2-second count-in), **R** again to stop
- Each take gets its own track with mute, solo, volume and an FX preset (Clean, Verb, Echo, Phone)
- **Export mix** or **Vocals only** as 44.1 kHz WAV

Wear headphones. On Bluetooth, raise the latency fix in Setup (150 to 250 ms is typical) or nudge each take with the timing buttons.

Everything stays in your browser. Nothing gets uploaded, and nothing is saved until you export.
